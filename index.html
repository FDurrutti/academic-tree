      How to add someone

      1. Find yourself in the tree. Type your name in the search box above the tree, or click your name in it.
      2. Click “+ Add chair” or “+ Add committee member”. A small form opens beside the tree.
      3. Type the names and click “Add to the tree”. They appear in the tree straight away, and everyone else sees them the next time they open the page.
      4. Keep going back. Click your chair to add their chair and committee, and so on.

      Tips

      • Solid lines lead to a person's chair, dotted lines to their committee members. Untick “Show committee members” to see only the chairs.
      • Write full names. Middle names and initials don't matter: “James S. Coleman” and “James Samuel Coleman” are the same person. Names already in the tree are suggested as you type.
      • Something wrong? Click the person and press “Remove” next to the wrong name, then add the right one. Every change can be undone: use “Undo” in the message that appears, or under “Recently added”.
      • Done with your own information? Click your name and tick “My information is complete and correct”, so we know. You can untick it anytime.
      • Name misspelled? Click the person and choose “Correct the name”. If two boxes are the same person, rename one to match the other and they merge.
      • Colors show each person's field, as listed on Academic Tree. Plain boxes are sociology. Wrong field? Click the person and choose “Change” next to Field.
      • The tree arranges itself: you only say who chaired or sat on whose committee, and the page works out where everyone goes.
